Reported target: iOS 16.3; black background both collapsed and expanded.
Tester model and jailbreak scheme are not yet known.

Cause and fix:
- The entire island constructor required iOS 17.0, so iOS 16.3 never installed it.
- Enable it on iOS 16+ only when all four required backing-view ivars exist.
  Add the missing _contentClippingView guard before any native-background hook.
- iOS 16.3 has _configureTransitionShadowViewIfNecessary, but lacks
  _layoutTransitionShadowView. Probe these separately; on the older layout,
  refresh shadow suppression after viewDidLayoutSubviews.
- iOS 17+ retains its dedicated shadow-layout callback when available.
