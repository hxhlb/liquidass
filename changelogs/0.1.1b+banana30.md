# 0.1.1b+banana30

## 中文

- 同步上游 0.1.1b，改进玻璃渲染、控制中心、通知、键盘、标签栏和解锁过渡。
- 新增经典／增强渲染器手动切换。
- 优化透明图标的主体、明暗细节和边缘，修复重复渲染、部分 Dock 图标仍保留原色的问题，并减少重复的图标玻璃底板。
- 改进锁屏玻璃时钟与日期显示，修复 iOS 15 时钟字号被日期标签覆盖，以及 iOS 18 时钟不显示、折射异常和 Rootless 字体路径问题。
- 优化运动高光更新，跳过相同角度和不可见视图；开启时提示可能出现卡顿、发热和额外耗电。
- 修复部分滑块重复触发事件造成的额外负载与卡顿。
- 加强渲染后端异常保护和诊断。

## English

- Integrates upstream 0.1.1b improvements to glass rendering, Control Center, notifications, keyboards, tab bars, and unlock transitions.
- Adds manual switching between Classic and Enhanced renderers.
- Refines clear icon subjects, shading, and edges; fixes duplicate processing and original-color Dock icons, and removes redundant individual glass backplates.
- Improves the glass lock screen clock and date display. Fixes date labels overwriting the iOS 15 clock size, missing clock rendering and refraction on iOS 18, and Rootless font resource lookup.
- Optimizes motion highlight updates by skipping unchanged angles and invisible views, with a lag, heat, and battery-use notice when enabled.
- Fixes repeated slider events that could cause unnecessary load and stuttering.
- Improves rendering backend failure protection and diagnostics.
