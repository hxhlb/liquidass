# 0.1.1b+banana31~widgets2

- 修复 iOS 18 电池等小组件的系统底板遮盖玻璃；底板重新创建、布局或显示更新时持续处理，关闭去背景时恢复原状态。
- 地图小组件跳过去背景和玻璃替换，保留地图画布与按钮；SpringBoard 与小组件渲染进程使用同一排除规则。
- 识别堆叠组件当前可见内容，避免缓存中的地图组件影响其他组件。
- Fixes native iOS 18 widget material covering the glass, including after recreation and visibility updates, with restoration when background removal is disabled.
- Excludes Maps widgets from background removal and glass replacement to preserve the map canvas and controls in both SpringBoard and the widget renderer.
- Uses the visible widget in a stack so cached offscreen Maps entries do not disable other widgets' glass.
